# Reference fight notes (2026-10-27)

From Don's 4 recordings of the reference game (videos 1 to 3 have battles; video 4 is exploration). These notes are what we **take example from**: the camera, pacing, scale and the techniques that make it feel good. We do not copy their creatures, models, arena art, UI artwork or their specific effect designs (the jaw bite, firework rocket and cyan splash rings). Our abilities get our own element looks, at the same scale and energy.

## Why theirs feels good and ours didn't
1. **Camera.** Their spirit stands big in the lower-left foreground, filmed from a low angle behind it, with the foe smaller at the upper right. **The camera cuts between shots during a turn:**
   - a wide shot past your spirit to the foe when the foe acts;
   - a close or low shot on the target when a big hit lands;
   - a slow drift the rest of the time.

   Ours was one static, far-away camera.
2. **Energy you can see.** Each charge adds yellow lightning bolts that keep orbiting the spirit, so the bolts show its energy. Using an attack makes the bolts fly off. Each side's energy pill shows "+1" and "-2" pops, and it flashes red when energy is spent.
3. **Huge effects.** A hit fills a big part of the screen:
   - dozens of streaks bursting up around the target;
   - rings spreading across the whole arena floor;
   - a bright bloom flash;
   - sparkles that keep drifting afterwards;
   - the target turning a solid red silhouette;
   - a big red damage number with a dark outline ("-27", "-53").

   Ours were small and faint.
4. **Projectiles have a body.** The big attack is a real object that flies in an arc with a fire trail, then explodes in bright colours.
5. **Readable turn text.** Blue pill bars at the bottom left, one per action ("X USED CHARGE!"), stacked for the whole turn.
6. **Menu.** FIGHT / BAG / (team) / RUN as big chunky coloured buttons with a red triangle cursor. FIGHT opens 4 moves (Charge plus 3 attacks, each showing its energy cost) and BACK.
7. **Endings.** The loser falls flat, then:
   - a white typewriter box ("X died!") with a red arrow;
   - a team XP panel slides in;
   - coins burst from the platform and fly into the coin counter.
8. **Pacing.** A turn is about 3 to 4 seconds, but something is always moving: bolts orbiting, the camera drifting and cutting, particles drifting. It never feels like waiting.
9. **Spirits are animated models** that idle-bounce, not orbs.

## What we build from this (our own content)
- **Camera:** closer and lower, our spirit big in the foreground, with cuts per action and a drift.
- **Energy as orbiting bolts** on both spirits.
- **Abilities:** each spirit has Charge plus 3 abilities of its own, each with a big element effect:
  - Fire: flames;
  - Nature: leaves and vines;
  - Earth: rocks bursting up;
  - Wind: swirls.
- **Hits:** a red silhouette, a big outlined damage number, a bloom flash and lingering sparkles.
- **Later:** real models, coins and XP (they need the economy), and the player avatar standing in the arena.
