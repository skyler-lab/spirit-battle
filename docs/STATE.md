# Spirit Battle: current state

Last updated: 2026-10-27 (battle feel pass with BattleFXPack)

## What it is
Roblox Prodigy-style spirit battle game. Working name "Spirit Battle", final name TBD. "Storm Spirits" is the old name and the old design (5-minute storm rounds, grab spirits, deliver to base); that design is dropped. Owner: Don (Roblox user SkylerH007). Not published. Halloween 2026 target.

Loop (planned): walk up to a wild elemental spirit, fight it in a 1v1 turn battle (energy 0 to 10, moves cost energy, Block saves energy), tame it when its health is low, keep it in your team, place it on your home base where it earns cash per second. Element advantage (fire beats nature, water beats fire, etc.). Islands themed by element, picked from a world map; fire island first. Later: shops, potions, cosmetics, base customizing, friendly player battles, evolution lines, rebirth.

Reference game: "Catch a Brainrot". Copy its mechanics, pacing, flow and layout as a base, not its creatures, models, arena art, UI art or effect art.

## How it is built
Battle (the new game, built and partly tested):
- `ReplicatedStorage.BattleData`: the one rule set. MaxEnergy 10, StartEnergy 1, +1 energy per turn each side. Moves: Charge (free, +2 energy), Strike (cost 2, 20 dmg), Block (cost 1, cancels one attack, refunds cost when it blocks), Blast (cost 5, 45 dmg). Tame (placeholders): works at or below 30% wild health; chance 25% at 30% health rising linearly to 90% near 0; `D.tameChance(hp, max)`; owned list cap 60. Test spirits Wood=Nature, Ember=Fire, Pebble=Earth, Breeze=Wind, 100 HP each. Elements: Fire>Nature>Earth>Wind>Fire, Water>Fire; 1.5x strong, 0.5x weak. `D.eventTime(e)` gives each event's play time so server and client stay in step (2026-10-27): say 0.45, charge 0.9, block 0.7, Strike hit 1.0, Blast hit 1.5, blocked 0.8, turn-end energy 0.2, end win 2.4 / lose 1.8 / tame 1.2 / run 0.4, tame try 4.4 success / 4.0 fail. All numbers are placeholders.
- `ServerScriptService.BattleServer`: spawns wild spirits on `Workspace.TestArea` WildSpot parts (Species attribute). Walk within 9 studs: "!" for 0.9s, player frozen, battle starts. Server-authoritative turns, random foe AI. Turn events play per spirit (2026-10-27): any Block goes up first, then your move, then the wild spirit's; each is its "used X!" line followed by its effect. A spirit knocked out before its move does not act (so a double knockout no longer happens; the result is the same win). arena built per player far away (4000+, 300, 4000) as a Persistent Model. Remotes in `ReplicatedStorage.BattleRemotes` (Start, Events, End, Move). Win: wild despawns, respawns after 8s. Lose or run: 4s cooldown. Placeholder rule: losing sends you to spawn with no penalty. In the arena only, a placeholder orb sits on its platform and is not neon (so hits land on it). Player's spirit is the player attribute BattleSpirit, default Wood. Sets player attribute InBattle while a battle runs (HealthServer, now disabled, skipped storm damage while it was set). TAME (move id "Tame"): server rejects it above the threshold; a failed try uses your turn and the wild spirit still acts; a success ends the battle ("You tamed X!"), removes the wild spirit like a win and appends its name to the player attribute OwnedSpirits. Studio-only test hooks (Workspace attributes, ignored outside Studio): TestFoeHpFrac (0 to 1, wild starting health), TestTameForce ("success" or "fail") and TestEndHold (seconds the last frame of a battle stays up, for screenshots). Teleport pads: part with a ProximityPrompt and a Dest attribute.
- `StarterPlayerScripts.BattleClient`: plain placeholder battle UI (foe panel, your panel with energy, message bars, FIGHT / BAG(soon) / TEAM(soon) / RUN, move page with costs, TAME button under the menu showing the live chance or "needs 30% HP or less"), fade, scripted camera (well behind and to the side of your spirit so both look closer in size, distances scale with the gap, FOV 40 in battle and restored after), hides other HUDs during battle, uses RequestStreamAsync. Since 2026-10-27 battle effects come from BattleFXPack (switch `USE_FX_PACK` at the top of the effects section; false goes back to the old plain effects, which are kept): intro camera circling the wild spirit then your spirit dropping in; idle bob and sway (SpiritRig); Charge bolts (pack); Strike = lunge and slash in the element colour; Blast = growing orb arcing over and exploding; Block = pack shield bubble that pops when hit; hits = red flash, element burst, pack damage number, target jolts back, camera punch and shake; big centre words (SUPER EFFECTIVE!, not very effective, BLOCKED!, TAMED!, IT BROKE FREE!); +/- energy pops by the panel; health bars drop when the hit lands; win = foe faints (models tip over, orbs drop), rises, fades and bursts into sparkles; tame = pack box throw, two wobbles, star burst or break free; lose = your spirit tips over. Menu buttons unchanged.
- `ReplicatedStorage.BattleFXPack` (folder, v0.2, 2026-10-22, from the look-and-feel pass): asset-free battle presentation built from Parts in code. Modules: FX (effects), Moves (ability library), BattleCamera (shots), BattleDialog (typewriter dialog, toasts, energy pops, team XP, level-up), BattleMenu, BattlePlay, Sequencer (Entry, SendOut, Turn, Faint, Catch, Swap, Exit), README, Demo. Everything runs on the client. BattleClient uses FX (Charge, HitFlash, DamageNumber, Sparkle, Puff, SparkleRain, Faint, CatchBox, StarBurst, Cleanup) and Moves (Shield, ShieldBreak); the Sequencer, BattleCamera, BattleDialog, BattleMenu and BattlePlay are not used. Changed 2026-10-27: FX.CatchBox screen tint is now light warm (was a dark red that hid the scene). Bite, Splash and Firework are left unused because they copy the reference game's effect art.
- `StarterPlayerScripts.Demo`: identical copy of BattleFXPack.Demo. DISABLED 2026-10-23 (Enabled = false, not deleted) because its arena, camera and dialog covered the real battle. The Enabled flag lives only in the place file, not in `src/`.

Base and spirits (keepers):
- `ServerScriptService.BaseServer`: 6 plots in `Workspace.GreyBox` (Plot1..6), 6 base slots per player (up to 9 with the Slots upgrade), spirits on slots earn cash per second, sell prompt on each spirit (60x per-second). Saves cash, slots, upgrades, coils, shields and owned spirits (`owned`, ";"-separated names from OwnedSpirits) to DataStore `StormSpiritsV1`; unpublished Studio uses an in-memory fake store. `ServerStorage.TestRejoin` BindableFunction tests save and load. Receives old storm-era deliveries through `ServerStorage.TryDeliver`. Placing tamed spirits: RemoteFunction `ReplicatedStorage.BaseAction` ("place", owned-list index) or ("take", slot). The server checks data loaded, own plot, within 90 studs of the plot centre (PLACE_RANGE placeholder), whole-number argument, the spirit is really in OwnedSpirits, a free slot. Place moves the name out of OwnedSpirits into the first free slot (rarity looked up from SpiritData.ByRarity, mutation None); take moves it back (capped by BattleData.Tame.MaxOwned). Player attribute PlacedSpirits ("slot|name|rarity;...") mirrors the base for the client. Placed spirits use the existing slot save, so they and cash restore on rejoin.
- `ReplicatedStorage.SpiritData`: rarities Common to Secret, cash per second 1, 3, 10, 30, 100, 300, 1000, mutations Big x3 and Shiny x2, placeholder roster (Wood, Breeze, Ember, Pebble, Nature, Light, Gale, Blaze, Boulder, Pumpkin, Mother Earth, Inferno, Tempest, Mountain, Void, Eclipse, Harvest Moon), zone weights from the storm era.
- `ReplicatedStorage.SpiritModels`: chunky multi-part models built in code, front faces -Z. Only Wood and Breeze have real models; others use BuildOrb recolored by element.
- `ReplicatedStorage.SpiritRig`: code-only animation for those models (walk, hover, idle).
- `ReplicatedStorage.UpgradeData` + `ServerScriptService.UpgradeServer` + `StarterPlayerScripts.ShopClient`: plain shop near `Workspace.GreyBox.CenterShop` (within 40 studs). Tracks Health, Bag, Slots, Income, coils, Shield item. All prices 0. Health, bag and shield may need rethinking under the pivot.
- `StarterPlayerScripts.OwnedSpiritsClient`: TEMPORARY plain panel on the left edge: "My spirits (N)" with a Place button per spirit, "On my base (N)" with a Take back button per slot, and a one-line result ("Done: placed Ember" / "Can't: go to your base first"). Hidden during battles.
