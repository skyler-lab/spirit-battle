## Left out on purpose
- **Packs named after anime characters** (Gojo, Sukuna, Rasengan, Hakari). They copy other people's characters' moves.
- **"not mine just saving" (synmade).** A re-upload of someone else's work.
- **Results whose thumbnail was blank** (several water, leaf and hit packs). Can't judge them.
- **Results that weren't effects** (vehicles, plants, furniture) that the search mixed in.

## Risks
- Free models can hide scripts that cause lag, spam or worse. That is why they get checked in a test place first.
- Packs from deleted accounts may be re-uploads of someone else's work. Pack A's description says "No scripts", but that is unverified.
- Toolbox effects are usually anime style: glowy, flashy and particle-heavy. The reference game uses flat, outlined cartoon shapes, so we would recolour and simplify them.
- None of these packs has a jaw, a rocket or a big vine, so those single big effect objects would still need to be made.
